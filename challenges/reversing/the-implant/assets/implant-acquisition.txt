Acquisition HX/MEM-4. Linux amd64 little endian ELF core; captured PT_LOAD segments have original virtual addresses. No filesystem offsets were preserved in pointers. The context validator accepts one contiguous context header at argv[1] and prints a structural diagnostic; it neither reconstructs fragments nor decrypts resume state. Utility: context-check <header-file>. Function names were removed from the recovered executable, but its interface and constant tables are intact. The circular event writer stores CRC32 on all preceding bytes of each record. Sequence numbers are monotonic but have gaps; physical ring position is unrelated to session order. Nonzero status means the operation did not complete. Operation families: 1 begin (handle), 2 module load (handle; flag bit0 signed), 3 connect (handle; flag bit0 approved), 4 stage (head pointer), 5 transmit (head pointer), 6 cleanup (handle). No other flag bits affect these decisions. Generation rollover is expected.
